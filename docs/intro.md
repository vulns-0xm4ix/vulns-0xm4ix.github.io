# > 0xM4IX

Personal knowledge base for offensive security research. Notes, techniques, and attack chains collected from labs, CTFs, and real-world assessments.

## What's here

- **Active Directory** — Kerberos abuse, ACL exploitation, AD CS attacks, delegation weaknesses, and domain dominance techniques.
- **Linux** — Privilege escalation, SUID abuse, cron jobs, kernel exploits, and post-exploitation.
- **Footholds** — Initial access techniques, shell stabilization, and enumeration methodology.
- **Methodology** — Structured approach to pentesting engagements from recon to reporting.

## How to use

Use the sidebar to navigate topics. Each page covers a specific technique or attack category with commands, tools, and notes from hands-on practice.

> These are personal research notes. Use responsibly and only on systems you have permission to test.
